How the Boo-ard Room works

A command station that designs its own interface from a messy incident report.

  1. Report. Paste a report or pick a preset on the left, then press Summon.
  2. Watch it think. The stages light up: the Intake Analyst reads the facts, the CrewAI Flow routes it, the Field Researcher checks the readings on You.com, and Parasail lays out the station.
  3. Read the station. The model chose every panel, its order and the theme. People in danger come first.
  4. Act. Pick a counter-measure and press Dispatch to alert #loft-incident.

Send another report to update the station in place, or click the timeline to rewind. Hover anything for an explanation.
